Community garage sale timeline: an 8 week plan for agents

Eight weeks gives you room to get permission, recruit homes, build the map, and promote the sale without rushing. Here is the outline, week by week.

See the kit Get the free guide

By Clark Richardson. Last updated November 10, 2026.

The short answer: start about 8 weeks before sale day. Spend the first two weeks on the date, permission, and your sign up form. Spend weeks 6 through 3 recruiting homes. Close signups about three weeks out, build the map, then spend the last two weeks promoting the sale to shoppers. Follow up starts the day after the sale.

The timeline at a glance

WhenFocusWhat gets done
Week 8Date and permissionChoose the streets, a Saturday, and a rain date. Contact the HOA board. Look up your city's garage sale and sign rules.
Week 7Approval and setupGet the board's go ahead in writing. Apply for any permit or note who must apply. Build the sign up form with a follow up consent checkbox. Start the map.
Week 6Recruiting startsAnnounce the sale through the HOA newsletter or email list and neighborhood groups. Leave recruiting flyers at front doors.
Week 5Keep recruitingPost a reminder. Answer questions from neighbors. Check how many homes have signed up.
Week 4Signup push and signsSend a second reminder with the signup deadline. Order or prepare signs.
Week 3Close signups and build the mapClose the form at the end of the week. Add every address to the map and make the printable version. Prepare the shopper flyer.
Week 2Promote to shoppersPost the sale in neighborhood groups, local buy and sell groups, and garage sale listing sites. Send participants the sale day details.
Week 1Final prepShare the map link. Put up signs only when your city allows. Check the forecast and decide on the rain date if needed.
Sale dayBe thereShare the map early, walk the neighborhood, meet homeowners, and keep signs standing. Take signs down when the sale ends.
AfterFollow upThank participants within a few days. Share how it went. Keep in touch with households that agreed to hear from you.

What people forget

  • A rain date. Pick it in week 8 and put it on the flyers so nobody has to guess.
  • Sign timing. Some cities only allow signs a short time before the sale. San Antonio, for example, allows them two days before. Check yours.
  • Leftover items. Decide early whether you will point people to a donation option, so unsold items do not sit on curbs.
  • The follow up message. Write the thank you note before sale day so it goes out on time.

Can you do it in less than 8 weeks?

You can, but recruiting suffers first. If you only have four or five weeks, get permission and the form done in the first week, keep a shorter recruiting window, and lean harder on the HOA's own channels. If the HOA needs a board vote that only happens monthly, start earlier than 8 weeks.

Questions we hear

How far in advance should you plan a community garage sale?

About 8 weeks. Start earlier if your HOA board only meets monthly or your city's permit takes time.

When should signups close?

About three weeks before the sale. That leaves time to build the map and promote the sale with a final list of homes.

When should promotion to shoppers start?

About two weeks out, with a reminder in the final days before the sale.
