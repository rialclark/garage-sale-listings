Is this a site for finding garage sales nearby?

No. This site is for real estate agents who want to organize a community garage sale. HOAs and neighborhood groups who want a sale run for them can go to communitysalehelp.com.

Does the kit work in every state?

The kit works for neighborhoods anywhere in the US. Local rules on permits and signs are different everywhere, so the plan includes checking your HOA documents and your city's rules.

How long does it take to plan a sale?

About 8 weeks. Our 8 week timeline shows what happens each week.

What does it cost to run a sale?

It depends on signs, printing, permits, promotion, and your time. See our guide to community garage sale cost factors.

Do you need the HOA's permission?

If the neighborhood has an HOA, talk to the board first and get approval in writing. Check your city's rules too. See HOA and city garage sale rules.

Why would a real estate agent run a community garage sale?

A community garage sale gives you a real reason to meet the homeowners who sign up, at their own front door on sale day. The kit includes the timeline, the sign up form, the map, flyers, signs, and follow up scripts for the people who agree to hear from you.

How do I get neighbors to take part?

Pick a date, get approval if the neighborhood has an HOA, then open a sign up form and get the word out with flyers and posts. Neighbors often wait to sign up until a date is set and the plan is already in motion.

Where should I advertise the sale?

Advertise in the places local shoppers already look, and put signs at the neighborhood entrances on sale day. The kit includes a where to post and when schedule, plus flyer and sign templates. Local rules on signs differ, so check your city and your HOA before you post them.

Can agents follow up with homeowners after the sale?

Yes, with the people who agree to it. Add a consent checkbox to your sign up form, respect opt outs, and check the Do Not Call rules before making calls.

What if it rains?

Pick a rain date when you pick the sale date and put it on your flyers. If your city issues permits, ask how it handles weather.

What is the difference between the kit and a done for you sale?

The kit is for real estate agents who want to run the sale themselves. A done for you sale is for HOAs and neighborhood groups that want us to plan and run it. The kit is $99 and is not on sale yet. Done for you sales are requested at communitysalehelp.com or with the form on our home page.
