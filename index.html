FAQ

Is this a site for finding garage sales near me?

No. This is for the people who organize a community sale: real estate agents, and HOAs or neighborhood groups who want one run for them.

What format is the kit in?

You get documents and templates you can copy and edit: the timeline, the sign up form, the map guide, flyer and sign templates for Canva, the posting schedule, and the follow up scripts.

Does the kit work outside San Diego?

Yes. The kit works in any neighborhood. Only the done for you service for HOAs is limited to San Diego North County.

How much time does a sale take to plan?

The timeline runs about 8 weeks from picking a date to sale day.

Our HOA already asked an agent to coordinate a sale. Does this still help?

Yes. The kit gives that agent a ready plan so the work goes faster. Or the HOA can request a done for you sale using the form above.

Who runs the HOA sales?
